Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes Giantix Studios' policies and practices regarding the collection, use and disclosure of information in connection with our services.

Information Collection

The app may collect certain information automatically, such as device type, network provider, IP addresses, advertising and other device identifiers, clicks on advertisements, diagnostic or performance information and information about the way you use the app. See "Automatic Data Collection and Advertising" section for examples of how this information is used.

Information Sharing

We will share your information with third parties only in the ways that are described in this privacy statement. We may disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with our trusted services providers who work on our behalf, do not have an independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement;
  • if Giantix Studios is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of its assets, you will be notified via email and/or a prominent notice on our website of any change in ownership or uses of this information, as well as any choices you may have regarding this information;
  • to advertisers and third-party advertising networks and analytics companies as described below under the Section entitled Automatic Data Collection and Advertising.

Automatic Data Collection and Advertising

This app is supported via advertising and collects data to help the app serve ads. We may work with analytics companies to help us understand how the app is being used, such as the frequency and duration of usage. We work with advertisers and third-party advertising networks, who need to know how you interact with advertising provided in the app which helps us keep the cost of the app free. Advertisers and advertising networks use some of the information collected by the app. These third parties may also obtain information such as IP addresses, advertising and device identifiers, technical device and network information, app usage and advertising interaction information, diagnostic or performance information, and non-precise location information in order to help analyze and serve targeted advertising on the app and elsewhere. If you would like to opt-out from third-party use of this type of information to help serve targeted advertising, please visit the section entitled "Opt-out" below.

For personalized advertising, the legal basis for processing for which Giantix Studios is responsible is your consent pursuant to Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. For non-personalized or limited advertising processed without consent, the legal basis for processing for which Giantix Studios is responsible is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is financing the app through advertising.

We currently work with the following advertising networks, analytics companies and service providers:

  • Google (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob (Privacy Policy)
  • Unity Ads (Privacy Policy)

Please refer to the privacy policies of these companies for information about their privacy practices.

Google Play Games Services

Some of our games use Google Play Games Services (PGS) for platform authentication and features such as achievements. Play Games Services provides a Player ID and shares Gamer Identity (Gamertag, avatar) with the game when a player authenticates. The Play Games Services SDK collects analytics and diagnostics data. Depending on the features used, additional Game Service data, such as unlocked achievements, may be collected. Where our processing of player data is necessary for the performance of a contract with the player, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Players can delete Play Games Services data for individual games through their Play Games profile. For more information about Google's data practices, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

International Transfers

Some service providers may process personal information outside the EEA or UK. Where such transfers are not covered by an applicable adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, are used. Further information about these safeguards is available on request.

Opt-out

You can stop future collection of information through the app by uninstalling the app. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile app marketplace or network.

To opt out of interest-based ads, please visit the following links:

  • Google opt-out: https://adssettings.google.com/authenticated
  • Unity Ads opt-out: Refer to the privacy policy available at: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy, where instructions on opting out are provided.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom (UK), there is an "Ad Settings" button available within the app's settings menu. By accessing this button, users can manage their ad preferences and withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

Links to Third Parties

The app may contain links (including via advertising) to third-party websites. These websites are governed by their own privacy policies which may be substantially different from ours. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those websites to understand how they may collect or use your personal information.
